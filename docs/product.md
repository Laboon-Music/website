# Product overview

## The product

Laboon is a **musician matchmaking app**: find people to play with, start a
band, meet musicians by instrument, style, level and location.

## Purpose of this website

The website is **not** the app. Before launch its job is to:

1. Present the concept attractively.
2. **Collect email addresses** for two lists:
   - be notified of the **launch**,
   - join the **beta** (testers).

A visitor can tick one option, the other, or both.

It also hosts companion pages for the app: legal documents (also embedded in
the app WebView), the web fallback of the password reset link, and the iOS /
Android deep link association files.

## Audience

Amateur and experienced musicians looking to play or collaborate with others.
Most traffic is expected **on mobile** (social networks) — hence the
mobile-first rule in [conventions.md](conventions.md).

## Success metrics

- Number of collected emails (launch / beta).
- Visitor → subscriber conversion rate (see [analytics](features/analytics.md)).

## Tone & look

Modern, energetic, community-driven; Laboon blue on night blue (see design-system.md). Informal French ("tu").
Make people want to be part of the adventure from day one.

## Owner

The project owner is **not a developer**: favour simplicity, explain manual
steps clearly (step-by-step, with where to click), automate everything that
can be automated.
